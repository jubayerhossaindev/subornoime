# BDS Certification Route & Legal Eligibility Investigation

**Mission**: COMPLETE BDS COMPLIANCE… (2026-11-24)
**Question answered honestly**: for standalone downloadable Windows software
(SubornoIME), what BSTI conformity assessment / certification is actually
possible today?

## 1. Legal framework (fully obtained — verbatim anchors)

The Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution Act, 2018 (Act No. LXVII of
2018; authentic English text published by S.R.O No. 322-Law/2020, 26 Nov 2020,
SRC-08). Key provisions captured verbatim:

**Section 6 — Functions of the Institution** (EV-A-01): BSTI sets up
Bangladesh Standards "of quality and dimensions", promotes adoption "on
national and international basis relating to commodities, products,
structures, practices and productions", secures producer/user compliance,
implements standards "through the administration of a national certification
mark scheme or inspection of goods or both", and issues test reports
"of any article relating to Bangladesh Standards".

**Section 15 — Prohibition of use of Standard Mark without licence** (EV-A-02):
"Except under a licence issued under section 16, no person shall use, in
relation to any article or process, or in the title of any patent, or in any
trade mark or design, the Standard Mark or any imitation thereof." And: no
use unless the article/process "conforms to the Bangladesh Standard".

**Section 16 — Licence** (EV-A-03): licence issued after inquiry where (a) the
article or process "conforms to the related Bangladesh Standard" and (b)
"there is arrangement for regular inspection or testing". Licence validity:
**three years**; revocable for condition violation.

**Section 18 — Prohibition of use of certain names** (EV-A-04): without prior
BSTI sanction, nobody may use names resembling "Bangladesh Standard" /
"Bangladesh Standard Specification" or marks containing those expressions.
→ This directly governs marketing language: SubornoIME must not advertise
itself as "Bangladesh Standard"-certified without sanction.

**Section 21 — Power to prohibit sale etc. of certain articles** (EV-A-05):
Government may, by Gazette notification, prohibit sale/distribution/advertisement
of non-conforming articles and may require Standard Mark marking on
conforming articles. The Act's article-oriented machinery (covers, labels,
receptacles in §21 Explanation) is physical-goods oriented.

**Reading**: the CM scheme is article/product-oriented (physical goods with
factory inspection, marking fee as % of ex-factory price in secondary
sources). Software-as-download does not obviously fit "article… cover… label"
machinery; the Act permits BSTI to certify "processes" as well (§15/§16 use
"article or process"), so a software conformity assessment is not textually
impossible — but no published software CM scheme was found in any official
source retrieved (SRC-01/09/10). Honest answer: **eligibility NOT CONFIRMED;
PENDING OFFICIAL RESPONSE** (inquiry drafts ready).

## 3. Answers to the twelve mission questions (PART 6)

1. **Within BSTI certification framework?** The Act's machinery is article/process oriented; software is not textually excluded, but no published software CM route was found. → UNCONFIRMED.
2. **Downloadable software in a CM scheme?** No evidence of such a scheme in retrievable official sources. → UNCONFIRMED.
3. **BDS 1738 software-applicable or device-scope?** The standard's full scope text is not readable; the BCC listing calls it the national computer keyboard layout. Foreword extract (brief) ties it to Bijoy and Unicode 10.0. Whether its scope covers software implementations of the layout (vs physical keyboards) cannot be honestly determined without the full document. → BLOCKED.
4. **Software conformance assessment possible for BDS 1520/1935?** BDS 1520 is character-repertoire/interchange — technically assessable via test evidence (this mission already produced executable repertoire/encoding evidence). Whether BSTI accepts such a dossier as formal conformance assessment: → inquiry item.
5. **Voluntary route?** Exists as a scheme (SRC-01); eligibility criteria unreadable. → BLOCKED.
6. **Product certification vs process assessment vs test report?** §6(f) test-report route plus §15/§16 licence route are the two textual mechanisms; mapping to software is unresolved.
7. **Recognized/accredited testing laboratory?** None found for software. → UNRESOLVED.
8. **BSTI assesses directly or designated labs?** For product CM, BSTI does inspection + testing per the CM scheme (SRC-01 navigation; §16(2)(b)). Software-specific arrangement unknown.
9. **Application documents?** Prescribed form per §16(1) and regulations; the actual form list is behind the CM process pages that render empty. → BLOCKED; checklist drafted conservatively.
10. **Fees and timeline?** Primary-source fee schedule not retrievable (fee pages exist in navigation). Secondary 2012-era figures (application BDT 50, licence BDT 200/yr, marking 0.07% ex-factory) are **dated and goods-oriented — flagged UNVERIFIED**. Timeline: §16 licence validity 3 years is the only verbatim anchor.
11. **Evidence/sample submission?** §16(2)(b) requires "regular inspection or testing" arrangement; software equivalents (build, test dossier) to be proposed in inquiry.
12. **Certificate scope wording?** Governed by licence form/conditions (§16(3)); not readable. Conservative scope statement drafted in the readiness dossier.
